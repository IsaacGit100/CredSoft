Best Practices:
  • Headings (e.g., ASSETS, LIABILITIES): Leave "Data Entry Account" UNCHECKED
  • Group Accounts (e.g., CASH AND CASH EQUIVALENTS): Leave UNCHECKED
  • Leaf Accounts (e.g., Cash in Hand, Member Savings): CHECK "Data Entry Account"
  • Once an account has ledger entries, it cannot have sub-accounts added
Note: The "Data Filled" flag is automatically set when ledger entries exist. Once set, no sub-accounts can be added to this account.
